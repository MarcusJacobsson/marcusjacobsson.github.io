Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2025

Overview

TheMediaPlayer2 ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains how our app handles your information.

Data Collection and Storage

TheMediaPlayer2 does not collect, store, or transmit any personal user data. The app:

  • Does not require user accounts or registration
  • Does not collect personal information
  • Does not store user data on external servers
  • Only accesses music files locally on your device for playback purposes

Permissions

The app requests the following permissions:

  • Storage/Media Access: Required to read and play music files stored on your device. This data is only used locally and is never transmitted or stored elsewhere.

Analytics

We may use Google Analytics or similar services to collect anonymous usage statistics to help improve the app. This may include:

  • App usage patterns and features used
  • Device type and operating system version
  • Crash reports and performance data

This information is collected anonymously and cannot be used to identify individual users.

Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services (such as Google Analytics) that may collect information used to identify you. Please refer to their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy

Data Security

Since we do not collect or store personal data, there is no risk of your personal information being accessed, disclosed, altered, or destroyed. All music playback occurs locally on your device.

Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13 years of age. Since we do not collect personal data, the app is safe for users of all ages.
