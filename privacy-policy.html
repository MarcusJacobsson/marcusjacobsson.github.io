Privacy Policy

Yggdrasil: Ascension · Last updated: 6 November 2026

This policy describes how the Android game Yggdrasil: Ascension (the “App”), published by Chincheck Software (“we”, “us”), handles information.

In short: we do not collect, sell or share any personal data. The App plays offline and has no ads, analytics or tracking. If you sign in to Google Play Games, Google keeps your achievements, leaderboard scores, game stats and a backup of your progress in your Google account; see Google Play Games below.

Information we collect

We do not collect any information. The App sends nothing to us, and it contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. The only service it talks to is Google Play Games, as described below.

The App does not ask for, and has no access to, your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, camera, microphone, device identifiers or any other personal information.

Data stored on your device

To let you resume a game and see your history, the App saves the following in its private storage on your device:

  • your current run in progress (map, deck, combat state);
  • your game statistics and history of past runs;
  • game settings, such as sound preferences.

This data contains no personal information and is not accessible to us. It leaves your device only if you sign in to Google Play Games, as described below. You can delete it at any time by clearing the App’s storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the App.

Android backup

If you have turned on backup for your Google account, Android may include the App’s saved game data in your device backup and restore it when you set up a new device. This backup is performed by Android and Google under your account settings, not by us, and we have no access to it. It is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy, and you can turn it off in your device’s backup settings.

Google Play

The App is distributed through Google Play. When you download it, Google may collect information under its own terms; see Google’s Privacy Policy. We may receive aggregated, anonymous statistics from Google Play Console (such as install counts) that cannot identify you.

Google Play Games

The App uses Google Play Games Services. If you sign in to Google Play Games, Google signs you in to the App with your Play Games profile, and the App sends the following to Google, linked to that profile:

  • Achievements: which achievements you have earned and how far you are towards the others.
  • Leaderboard: your best score in the weekly climb, shown on the weekly leaderboard with your Play Games name.
  • Game stats: a summary of each finished run (such as the outcome, floors climbed, foes slain and play time) and the highest Ascension level you have unlocked, which Google adds up into the stats on your Play Games profile.
  • Cloud save: your progress (unlocks, Compendium records and the history of finished runs) is saved to Saved Games in your Google account, so you can continue on another device or after reinstalling. The run in progress stays on your device.

The App sends nothing else, and never your settings.

This sign-in and the data above are handled by Google under your Play Games settings and Google’s Privacy Policy. We do not receive your name, email address or any other personal information from it. Google Play Console may show us aggregated, anonymous figures, such as how many players have earned an achievement. We cannot read your saved games.

Play Games is optional. If you do not use it, or sign out of it, the game plays the same, and nothing is sent or backed up. You can see, change or delete your Play Games profile and its data in the Google Play Games settings.

Sharing and selling of data

We do not share, sell, rent or trade any data, because we do not collect any. Your Play Games data is held by Google, as described above.

Children’s privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Since no data is collected, no personal information from children is gathered or stored by us.

Security

Saved game data is kept in the App’s private storage, which Android protects from other apps.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. All game data on your device is under your control and can be removed as described above, and your Play Games data is managed in your Google Play Games settings.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the App changes how it handles data, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above before that version is released.
