Yggdrasil: Ascension

A Norse roguelike deckbuilder for Android

Climb Yggdrasil, the World Tree, from its roots to its crown. Every run lays out a new path up the tree, through fights, events, the Forge and the Mead Hall, to the beasts of Norse myth waiting at the top of each act.

Swing the axe as the Einherjar, a fallen warrior of Valhalla, or weave fate as the Völva, a seeress whose spirit Ward fights at her side. Build a deck from the cards you earn on the way, and make every choice count: each one has a price.

The game is played solo and works fully offline, with no ads and no in-app purchases.
