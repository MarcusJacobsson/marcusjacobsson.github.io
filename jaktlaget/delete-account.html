Delete your Jaktlaget account

This page explains how to delete your account in Jaktlaget, the hunting team management app by ChinCheck Software, and what happens to your data when you do.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Jaktlaget and sign in.
  2. Open the menu and go to Settings (Inställningar).
  3. Tap Profile (Profil).
  4. Tap Delete Account (Ta bort konto) and confirm.
  5. Enter your password to confirm. Your account is deleted immediately.

Request deletion by email

If you can no longer sign in to the app, send an email to chinchecksoftware@gmail.com from the email address you registered with, with the subject "Delete my Jaktlaget account". We will delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your login (email address and password) in Firebase Authentication
  • Your user profile: display name, email address and selected team
  • Your access to every hunting team you were a member of

What is kept

  • Hunting records that belong to a team you were in (harvests, allocations, member names, animal types) stay with that team, since they are shared with the other team members. They are deleted when the team is deleted. If you want your name removed from a team's records, ask the team administrator or contact us at the address above.
  • Data is not kept in backups or for any additional retention period after deletion.

Questions? Contact us at chinchecksoftware@gmail.com.
