Lamplight Deep

A turn-based digging roguelike for Android

Read the clues, dig by lamplight, and ride the last cage up with your gold. Every block holds something: glints mean ore, cracks mean a hidden cave, heat means lava, scratches mean something that bites. Your lamp shows the clues around you, and every dig costs oil.

The lift company wants its cut. Pay every quota, pack your tool belt each morning, collect relics that change how you dig, and break the glowing Core at the bottom of the mine. It's played with one thumb, in short dives of a few minutes.

The game is played solo and plays fully offline, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Signing in with Google Play Games is optional. If you sign in, the game uses your Play Games profile to unlock achievements, post your scores to the leaderboards, add your games to your Play Games stats, and back up your progress to your Google account’s Saved Games so you can continue on another device. It asks for nothing else, and nothing is shared with anyone. See the privacy policy for details.
