Privacy Policy

Lamplight Deep · Chincheck Software · Last updated 16 November 2026

This policy describes how the Android game Lamplight Deep (se.chinchecksoftware.lamplightdeep, the “App”), made by Chincheck Software (“we”, “us”), handles information.

In short: we do not collect, sell or share any personal data. The App plays offline and has no ads, analytics or tracking. If you sign in to Google Play Games, Google keeps your achievements, leaderboard scores, game stats and a backup of your progress in your Google account; see Google Play Games below.

Information we collect

We do not collect any information. The App sends nothing to us, and it contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. The only service it talks to is Google Play Games, as described below.

The App does not ask for, and has no access to, your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, camera, microphone, device identifiers or any other personal information.

What stays on your device

To let you continue a game and track your progress, the App saves two files in its own private storage on your phone:

  • Your current game: the state of the mine, your miner, relics and gold.
  • Your progress: lifetime statistics, unlocked miners and expedition tiers, a history of your most recent finished games, and settings such as sound volume.

These files contain no personal information and we cannot access them. They leave your device only if you sign in to Google Play Games, as described below. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data deletes them.

Android backup

If you have turned on backup for your Google account, Android may include the App’s saved game data in your device backup and restore it on a new device. This is done by Android and Google under your account settings, not by us, and we have no access to it. It is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy, and you can turn it off in your device’s backup settings.

Permissions

The App uses vibrate for haptic feedback during play, and internet and network state access, used only by Google Play Games. It does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage access.

Google Play

The App is distributed through Google Play. When you buy or download it, Google may collect information under its own terms; see Google’s Privacy Policy. We may receive aggregated, anonymous statistics from Google Play Console (such as install counts) that cannot identify you.

Google Play Games

The App uses Google Play Games Services. If you sign in to Google Play Games, Google signs you in to the App with your Play Games profile, and the App sends the following to Google, linked to that profile:

  • Achievements: which achievements you have earned and how far you are towards the others.
  • Leaderboards: your best scores, such as the deepest point you have reached, shown on the game’s leaderboards with your Play Games name.
  • Game stats: a summary of each finished game (such as the miner, the result, the day it ended, the depth reached and the gold banked) and the highest expedition tier you have unlocked, which Google adds up into the stats on your Play Games profile.
  • Cloud save: your progress (statistics, unlocked miners, expedition tiers and the history of finished games) is saved to Saved Games in your Google account, so you can continue on another device or after reinstalling. The game in progress stays on your device.

The App sends nothing else, and never your settings.

This sign-in and the data above are handled by Google under your Play Games settings and Google’s Privacy Policy. We do not receive your name, email address or any other personal information from it. Google Play Console may show us aggregated, anonymous figures, such as how many players have earned an achievement. We cannot read your saved games.

Play Games is optional. If you do not use it, or sign out of it, the game plays the same, and nothing is sent or backed up. You can see, change or delete your Play Games profile and its data in the Google Play Games settings.

Sharing and selling of data

We do not share, sell, rent or trade any data, because we do not collect any. Your Play Games data is held by Google, as described above.

Children

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Since we collect no data, no personal information from children is gathered or stored by us.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. Game data on your device is under your control and can be removed as described above, and your Play Games data is managed in your Google Play Games settings.

Changes

If a future version of the App changes how it handles data, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above before that version is released.
