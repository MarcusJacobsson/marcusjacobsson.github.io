Privacy Policy

Lamplight Deep and Lamplight Deep Demo · Chincheck Software · Effective 11 November 2026

This policy covers the Android games Lamplight Deep (se.chinchecksoftware.lamplightdeep) and Lamplight Deep Demo (se.chinchecksoftware.lamplightdeep.demo), made by Chincheck Software.

The short version

The game collects nothing. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no network access. Nothing you do in the game is sent to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

To let you continue a run and track your progress, the game saves two files in its own private storage on your phone:

  • Your current run – the state of the mine, your miner, relics and gold.
  • Your progress – lifetime statistics, unlocked miners and expedition tiers, and a history of your most recent finished runs.

These files contain no personal information. They never leave your device unless you copy them off it yourself, and Android's own device backup may include them if you have backup turned on. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes them.

Permissions

The game asks for one permission, vibrate, used only for haptic feedback during play. It does not request internet, location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage access.

Third parties

The game contains no third-party SDKs that collect data: no advertising, analytics, crash reporting or social login. Purchases and downloads are handled by Google Play under Google's privacy policy; we receive no personal data from Google about you beyond what Google Play shows developers for sales reporting.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. A change that starts collecting data would also come with an app update that tells you so.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer email address shown on the game's Google Play store page.
